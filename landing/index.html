How it works

From your question to a sourced report.

  1. 01
    You edit the questions. The search waits. Add one, cut one, rewrite one.
  2. 02
    Athel reads the sources. Athel searches each question, reads the page, and follows a PDF when the page points at one.
  3. 03
    You get a report you can check. Names, dates, and numbers stay attached to the link they came from.
